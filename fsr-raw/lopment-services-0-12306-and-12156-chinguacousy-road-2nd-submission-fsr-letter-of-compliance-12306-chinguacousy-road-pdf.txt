Argo Mayfield West II Limited

Carmine,
We confirm that the proposed development, Argo Mayfield West II Limited, as shown on the latest draft
plan prepared by GSAI, complies with the recommendations for site servicing, grading, drainage, and
stormwater management as described in the Alloa Tertiary Plan – Phase 1 Functional Servicing Report
(FSR) (September 2024).
Sanitary Servicing
The site is accommodated by the existing 750 mm sanitary trunk sewer on Brisdale Drive. Wastewater
generated within the draft plan area will generally flow east /west through local sewers to a proposed 375
mm sewer running north to south on Street D (Alexander Gillespie Ave). The Street D sewer will connect
to a proposed 450 mm sewer on Street B (Tim Manley Avenue), which flows west to the planned 525 / 675
mm regional trunk system running north to south on Speersville Avenue (Street E) (Project 29-2199). This
sewer ultimately discharges to the existing 750 mm stub connection at Brisdale Drive, just north of Mayfield
Road.
Water Servicing
Municipal water will be supplied to the Argo Mayfield West II Limited site through Peel Region’s lake-based
system (Pressure Zone 7W). Water services will extend north from the existing 600 mm watermain on
Mayfield Road and east from the existing 600 mm watermain on Chinguacousy Road. The Region’s capital
program includes two new 400 mm watermains—on Creditview Road (Project 26-1188) and Tim Manley
Boulevard (Project 26-1187)—to service the Alloa Phase 1 area, including the subject development. A
network of local 300 mm watermains (including on Alexander Gillespie Avenue – Street D) provide looping
and security of supply, connecting to both existing and proposed Regional infrastructure.

Stormwater Management
The Argo Mayfield West II draft plan is within the Etobicoke Creek watershed, under TRCA jurisdiction.
Post-development drainage patterns have been designed to closely match pre-development conditions.
Accordingly, stormwater associated with the draft plan is managed within the Etobicoke Creek watershed.
•

The majority of stormwater drainage flows north to SWM Pond 3, with outlet to the re-aligned Alloa
Drain.

•

The Mid-High Density Residential Block adjacent to Chinguacousy Road on the draft plan is
proposed to be controlled by a private on-site SWM facility, discharging to a new clean water storm
sewer on Chinguacousy Road. There is an existing clean water storm sewer connection on
Mayfield Road which runs east to an existing culvert. The approved drainage plans for both the
Mayfield West Phase 2 and Mount Pleasant Block 51-2 lands include provision for drainage from
this area of Alloa to the clean water pipe on Mayfield Road.

All ponds within the Alloa Secondary Plan are designed to meet applicable requirements for quantity,
erosion, and quality control (Enhanced Level 1 – 80% TSS removal).
Supporting Materials
Servicing drawings from the FSR are attached for information. For additional details please refer to the
Alloa Tertiary Plan – Phase 1 Functional Servicing Report (FSR) (September 2024). Please note that minor
variations in lotting and unit counts are not anticipated to impact the servicing plan as described in the FSR.
Servicing details will be finalized in consultation with the Town as part of detailed design.
