INTRODUCTION

This functional servicing and stormwater management Report has been prepared in support of a proposed
development located in the Town of Caledon. The 40.68 ha subject land is identified as Part of lot 13,
concession 7, in the Bolton community and is located east of Highway 50, north of Columbia Way and
west of Mount Hope Road, as shown in Figure 1.1.
This report summarizes and assesses the municipal servicing requirements for potable water, sanitary
servicing, and stormwater management. The following sections outline the proposed services for the
development and their capabilities with existing infrastructure within the Bolton North Hill area.

WATER SUPPLY SERVICING

This section describes the proposed potable water distribution system for the subject site. The site will be
serviced by the Town of Caledon's planned municipal water system. Detailed analysis, including hydraulic
modelling and hydrant flow testing, will be completed at the design stage.

The subject site is currently not serviced by the municipal water distribution system. Existing watermain
infrastructure within the surrounding road network consists of the following:
•

300 mm PVC distribution watermain on Columbia Way (Pressure Zone 6)

•

300 mm PVC distribution watermain on Regional Road 50 (Pressure Zone 6)

These watermains form part of the existing Pressure Zone 6 (PZ6) system. Due to the site's elevation
exceeding the serviceable range of PZ6 (214.5 m – 259.1 m), servicing from the existing system is not
feasible. Water servicing for the proposed development will be provided through the planned Pressure
Zone 7 (PZ7) system, as described in Section 3.3.
Future water servicing infrastructure is planned along Regional Road 50 and Columbia Way to support the
development of the Bolton North Hill area. The existing and proposed water servicing framework is
illustrated on Figure 3.1.

The water servicing system will be designed in accordance with the Region of Peel Public Works
Watermain Design Criteria (June 2010). The applicable design criteria are summarized below.

STORMWATER MANAGEMENT

This section describes the stormwater management strategy for the proposed development area,
including existing drainage conditions, applicable design criteria, and the proposed stormwater
management facilities.

The subject site is located east of Regional Road 50 (Highway 50), north of Columbia Way, and west of
Mount Hope Road, within the Humber River watershed and subject to the regulatory jurisdiction of the
Toronto and Region Conservation Authority (TRCA). Under existing pre-development conditions, the site
consists primarily of agricultural land with limited formal drainage infrastructure. Surface runoff is
conveyed via natural drainage channels and sheet flow to tributaries of the Main Humber River.
The site drains to two distinct tributary systems:
•

Tributary E (sub-basin 10): The southern and eastern portion of the site drains to Tributary E,
located within sub-basin 10 of the Humber River watershed.

•

Tributary D (Main Humber River): The northern and central portion of the site drains to Tributary
D of the Main Humber River.

Soils within the site are predominantly clay-based (including King Clay Loam), which limits infiltration
potential. Groundwater levels are relatively shallow and may influence stormwater management design.
No formal storm sewer infrastructure currently exists within the subject area. The pre-development
drainage catchment layout is depicted in Figure 5.1.

The stormwater management design for the subject site has been developed in accordance with the
following governing documents and criteria:

The proposed stormwater management strategy for the subject lands has been developed in accordance

adjacent lands. The final pond configurations, outlet structures, storage volumes, and associated drainage
channels will be confirmed during detailed design.
