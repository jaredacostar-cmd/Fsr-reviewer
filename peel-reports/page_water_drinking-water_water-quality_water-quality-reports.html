    Water quality reports

    Our annual water quality reports detail the operation of our drinking water system and drinking water quality test results.

    Drinking Water in Peel Summary Report

    Peel Region provides safe, clean drinking water to residents and businesses across Peel while meeting all Government of Ontario drinking water requirements.

    Peel Region’s drinking water systems have sufficient capacity to meet current demand and support future growth, reflecting our ongoing commitment to protecting public health and maintaining high water quality standards.

    • 2025 Drinking Water in Peel Summary Report

    2025 water quality annual reports

    • Palgrave, Caledon East, Centerville, and Cedar Mills (Palgrave – Caledon East Drinking Water System)
    • Caledon Village, Alton, Mono Mills, and Skywood (Caledon Village – Alton Drinking Water System)
    • Cheltenham, Terra Cotta and Poltawa (Cheltenham Drinking Water System)
    • Inglewood (Inglewood Drinking Water System)
    • Brampton, Mississauga and South Caledon (South Peel Drinking Water System)

    If you require previous years’ reports contact our Water Quality and Compliance team.
