    Design, standards specification and procedures

    The design of municipal services in Peel is based on our design, standards & procedures.

    These documents are intended to assist consultants, engineers, inspectors, and regional staff involved in the design and delivery of Regional Infrastructure and Services, as well as those involved in submitting engineering proposals and agreements for land development.

    Specifications and procedures listed here might not apply in all circumstances. Communicate directly with the appropriate Regional staff to consider variances on a case by case basis. Engineers are still responsible for ensuring that a design meets all federal, provincial, regional and local government requirements. This review does not release engineers from this responsibility.

    The Region shall review and approve all plans before construction starts. 

    Updating the manual

    The Region will periodically review and update the information within these documents based on current information and needs. To the best of its ability the Region will ensure that the documents made available ae the most recent version of the document(s) being utilized by the Region. The Region can not guarantee that all the information within these manuals is accurate, complete, or current at all times.

    These documents shall be read in conjunction with any project or contract documentation. If any discrepancies are noted between the documentation the more stringent requirement shall prevail unless otherwise stated within the project documentation or contract.

    The latest revision date will be clearly stated on each of the documents listed. Updated versions of the manual will include new products that the Region’s Technical & Materials Review Committee has accepted. Anyone utilizing any listed documentation shall be responsible to confirm that they are utilizing the most recent versions of the documentation. Contact your Regional constituent directly to confirm.

    Alternate formats

    All proposed new materials for use in Peel Region's construction and maintenance projects must be submitted for rigorous evaluation and approval by the Technical and Materials Review Committee. This process ensures adherence to Peel standards for performance, durability, and environmental sustainability. For inquiries or to initiate a material review, contact Jimmy Cheema and Denis Lefebvre.

    Region of Peel standard drawings illustrate Regional requirements and construction practices for the installation of municipal works.

    The corresponding Ontario Provincial Standard Drawing (OPSD) applies to areas that do not have an applicable Regional standard drawing.

    These standard drawings apply to only work on municipal property:

    • Watermain and appurtenances
    • Sanitary sewer and appurtenances
    • Within proximity of LRT
    • Electrical traffic standards
    • Roads and traffic

    If work will take place on a private property, contact the appropriate local municipality for standard drawings.
