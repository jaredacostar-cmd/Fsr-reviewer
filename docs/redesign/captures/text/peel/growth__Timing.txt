ⓘ Timing forecast — a statistical screening estimate, not a prediction for any one site. Approval and completion rates and the time lags are calibrated from the dated application and permit events in the municipal files since 2016, by municipality and building type (cohorts 2016–2019, so every project had 5+ years of follow-up; 11,265 dated observations; Wilson 95% intervals; cells with fewer than 10 observations fall back to coarser aggregates). Rates count a project as approved / completed from its current phase where the dated event is missing (dated completion events are sparse outside Mississauga); lags use dated events only. LOW uses the slow lags (p75) with the low rate bound, LIKELY the medians, HIGH the fast lags (p25) with the high bound. Trigger years phase each facility's approved / proposed flow layers in over windows from the calibrated lags and scale them by the calibrated rates — they are not the plant Horizon assumptions on the Wastewater tab. Calibrated on all projects; the forecast covers the developments shown. Applications as of 2026-10-12.
